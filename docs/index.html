Research internship, Politecnico di Torino

Conduct an orchestra from a VR headset.Musicians follow in their browser.

MEVO is a networked music performance system from Prof. Cristina Rottondi's group at the Department of Electronics and Telecommunications. Since May 2026 I've worked with Dr. Leonardo Severi and Dr. Matteo Sacchetto, who built the original system, to grow its shared rehearsal hall across the headset, the browser and the services between them.

What I built

One hall, two worlds

The rehearsal hall, with paintings, instruments, picture lamps and softer light, looks the same in the headset and in the browser.

Avatars musicians choose

Six avatars with outfit variants. Musicians pick one on the web page and the conductor sees it in VR.

A seating plan

Taken seats show on the plan, and a button lets a musician change seat.

Presence

Name labels over every musician, and heads that turn toward the conductor.

Musicians who rehearse

Idle motion between pieces. When the rehearsal timer runs, avatars take up their instruments with arm IK.

Connection quality in the headset

The conductor sees each musician's round-trip time, so a weak link is visible during the rehearsal.

Unity, C#, Meta Quest, Unity WebGL, TypeScript, Node.js, Fastify, WebRTC and WebSocket. The project is not public, so this page shows how it works instead of screenshots.

armap, a Claude Code plugin

Your AI agent is lost in your codebase.Give it a map.

armap ranks files with PageRank over their dependency graph and gives the agent a map of about 700 tokens before it starts. It needs no compiler, no language server and no runtime dependencies.

Jetaime

A shop window for a pastry brand.Its costs stay behind the counter.

A Persian website for a pastry brand, plus internal tools for costs, price lists and invoices. Both share one monorepo and one database.

The website can't reach costs

It connects with a restricted database role that reads published products only.

Prices that keep up

Ingredient prices are stored by date, so a new price updates the whole price list.

Invoices keep their numbers

Each invoice line stores its own price and cost, so old invoices don't change.

Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Drizzle and Turborepo. In development and not public yet, so the photos are placeholders from the brand book. Source code is private.
